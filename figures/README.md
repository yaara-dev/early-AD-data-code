# Supplementary figures

## Fig. S2 — Comparison of ExSeq data with the Allen Mouse Brain Atlas

File: [`Fig.S2.pdf`](Fig.S2.pdf)

Qualitative comparison of spatial gene expression in ExSeq sample `WT_2` with in situ hybridization patterns from the Allen Mouse Brain Atlas. ExSeq panels show DAPI (gray) and detected transcripts (blue); Allen Atlas panels show FISH signal (purple = high expression). Genes marked with an asterisk (*) were matched to sagittal Atlas sections (horizontally flipped) when coronal sections were unavailable. Spatial patterns agreed well with the Atlas references, supporting ExSeq data validity.
